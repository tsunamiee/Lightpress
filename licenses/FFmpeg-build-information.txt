FFmpeg for Lightpress

Lightpress uses FFmpeg 7.1.5 built from the archives locked in
tools/ffmpeg/sources.json. The build recipe is tools/ffmpeg/build.py.

License: GPL v3 or later.

Corresponding-source release asset:
FFmpeg-7.1.5-Lightpress-source.zip

The portable package includes its actual build configuration, toolchain
versions and binary checksums in licenses/FFmpeg-build-information.json.
FFmpeg-SOURCE.txt identifies the matching source asset and its SHA-256.
Dependency notices are in licenses/FFmpeg-dependencies.txt.
